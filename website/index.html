One little first step.

This preview is locally signed and isn’t notarized by Apple yet. macOS may ask you to approve it the first time.

  1. Unzip the download and move Clementine to Applications.
  2. Open Clementine. If macOS blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway after checking the app’s source.
  3. Look for the orange icon in your menu bar. Drag a file and tap Shift to convert, Command for tools, or Control for actions.
