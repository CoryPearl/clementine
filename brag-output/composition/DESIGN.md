# Clementine film identity

## Style Prompt
Use the app's orange radial glass wheel as the main object on a warm cream stage. Large editorial type gives the copy room; the demonstrated interaction provides the motion. This follows the user's orange / Liquid Glass direction and the native application's existing cream and serif identity.

## Colors
- #FFF4E5 — ground
- #4A291A — all text
- #F75C14 — interaction and brand accent
- #FFD9B5 — glass and file surfaces

## Typography
EB Garamond 600 display; Inter 400/600 body and UI. Headlines 100–116px, body 34–40px, safe margins 120px.

## What NOT to Do
No neon, dark void, particles, fake feature statistics, unreadable microcopy, permanent floating titles, or stock dashboards. Cream, serif and glass are deliberate matches to Clementine's source, not unrelated decoration.
